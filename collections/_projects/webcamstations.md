Webcams, weather and trail maps of more than 400 ski resorts on Windows 10.

> **No longer available**: WebcamStations has been removed from the Microsoft Store and is no longer maintained.

## Features

- Watch the live webcams of more than 400 ski resorts
- 5-day weather forecast with snow probability
- Trail map of each resort
- Direct access to the resort's website
- Save your favorite resorts for quick access
- Pin a resort to your Start screen
- Share webcams with your friends
