The family recipe book

All your recipes in one place, found in a second, and shared with the people you cook with. Add them by hand, import them from a web page, or just take a picture of a cookbook page and let the AI fill the recipe for you.

## Features

- **Your cookbook**: every recipe with its photos, ingredients, steps and tags.
- **Import from a link**: share a recipe page to the app, it fills the recipe for you.
- **Import from a photo**: snap a recipe from a book or a handwritten notebook, the AI finds its name, ingredients and steps.
- **With the family**: invite your relatives with a code, and all your recipes end up in one shared book.
- **Fast search**: find a recipe by its name, an ingredient or a tag, even offline. Typos are forgiven, and `-word` excludes a word.
- **Adjusted servings**: change the number of people, the quantities follow.

## Under the hood

- **App**: Flutter (iOS + Android), clean architecture in a Dart + Melos workspace, Riverpod and GoRouter.
- **Backend**: Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Storage, Cloud Functions in TypeScript, Hosting), with App Check enforced everywhere.
- **Search in the app**: a compact index per author stored in Firestore and kept in sync by a Cloud Function. 10 000 recipes fit in about 2 MB and 3 reads, and the scoring (tags, name, ingredients, prefix match, one typo tolerated) runs on the phone. It replaced a hosted search engine.
- **Photo import**: photos are resized in the app, then sent to a callable Cloud Function that asks Gemini Flash-Lite through Vertex AI, with a daily limit per user.
- **Families**: invitation codes checked by the server, and invitation links that open the app (Universal Links / App Links) or a web page with the store links.
- **Thumbnails** generated by a Cloud Function when a recipe cover is uploaded.
- **Security rules** tested on the Firebase emulators: a recipe is only visible to its author and their family.
