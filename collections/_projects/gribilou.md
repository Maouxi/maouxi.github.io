## Snap it, it's sorted

- The scanner finds the sheet, straightens it and crops it.
- A whole pile of drawings at once, or from your photos.
- Every drawing is sorted by child, age and date.
- Drawing, coloring, painting, crafts, school: find anything with the filters.

## Their voice

- "Tell me about your drawing!" Record up to 2 minutes of their voice on each drawing.
- In ten years, you'll hear again what your child said about it.

## The whole family

- Invite another parent, the grandparents or other relatives with a code.
- They get notified of each new drawing and can say "I love it".
- Relatives see everything, only parents make changes.
- Several kids? Each one has their own page, color and very first drawing.

## Memories that come back

- "One year ago, Léa drew…" One year later, to the day, Gribilou shows you the drawing again.
- Share a drawing on its own or in a nice frame, with its title and date.

## Your data

- The drawings are stored in France and shared only with your family.
- No ads, nothing is ever sold, and voices are never sent to any AI.
- Photo GPS location is removed before upload.
- Works offline: drawings upload as soon as you're back online.
- Nothing is ever deleted unless you ask: the full export (images and voices, as a ZIP) is always free.

## Free to start

- Free: 30 drawings per family, with every feature.
- Premium: unlimited drawings and several families, monthly or yearly.
- One subscriber is enough for the whole family. Joining a family is always free.
